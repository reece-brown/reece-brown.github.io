About

I am the Associate Director of Impact at the Human Flourishing Program at Harvard University, where I work on the application and translation of wellbeing science for anyone who will listen. I’m an expert in positive psychology and designing systems that optimize for human flourishing.

Current work

Among other things, I am currently focused on the Flourishing x Work Project, where we help leaders transform workplace cultures toward greater flourishing. I also focus on character and character leadership, entrepreneurship, and teaching the science of human flourishing.

Background

After an initial career in finance, I joined Arthur Brooks at the Harvard Business School and the Kennedy School of Government. I helped Arthur create new courses, books, and columns on the science of happiness. We launched the Leadership & Happiness Laboratory, where we delivered social impact ventures across the US and around the world.

I joined the Human Flourishing Program to continue the mission of lifting people up and bringing them together, and have had the opportunity to work on topics such as academic freedom, the Global Flourishing Study, an international research collaborative of campuses focused on character and virtue, and working with students through the Oxford Vivarium.

I received my bachelor’s in economics from Gordon College and a master’s in applied positive psychology from the University of Pennsylvania.
